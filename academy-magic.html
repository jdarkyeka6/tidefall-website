MAGIC SYSTEM GUIDE

Tidefall Academy Magic

Magic at Tidefall Academy is cast without wands. Students learn to produce, control and refine magical effects through practice, spoken spells and hand-cast magic.

Wandless spellcasting

Tidefall magic is deliberately not wand-based. A student's challenge is not finding the correct wand but learning control, intention, strength and precision.

Different strengths

Harper Vale tends toward power before precision. Ava Rees is strongest when accuracy matters and improves through testing. Jasper Holloway is analytical and pattern-focused. Lily Hart's magic can become especially intense under pressure.

The Tide

The Tide is not simply another spell. It behaves as a strange force capable of carrying, choosing and interfering. The Choosing Tide, used in Order placement, is one of the clearest examples.

Titan-sense

Titan-sense is exceptionally rare. Jasper Holloway and Lily Hart possess the fuller form: they can sense Titans, hear them and communicate with them. The Headmaster has a more limited form connected to hearing rather than the complete connection.

Spells

The Academy teaches practical magic, while the official Tidefall Spells & Magic page contains named spells and interactive spell content. Not every magical phenomenon is a normal classroom spell.
