About

Ava is methodical without being cold. She notices inconsistencies, records details other people forget, and often gives the group a clearer picture of what actually happened after everyone else has finished arguing about it.

Magic

Ava is strongest when accuracy matters. She tends to refine technique quickly and treats magical progress almost like a problem that can be measured, tested and improved.

Signature

An old brass key is Ava's signature object. Green is the colour most closely associated with her throughout Tidefall's visual identity.

Connections

Ava is part of the central group with Jasper Holloway, Lily Hart and Harper Vale. She is often the person checking whether everyone else's brilliant idea survives contact with basic logic.
