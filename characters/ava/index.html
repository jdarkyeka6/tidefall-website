Ava Rees is a fictional character in Tidefall, the fantasy universe centred on Tidefall Academy. She is one of Tidefall's four central characters alongside Jasper Holloway, Lily Hart and Harper Vale. Precise, observant and suspicious of badly labelled systems, Ava likes facts, patterns and getting things exactly right.

Who is Ava Rees?

Ava Rees is a main Tidefall character and Brannor student at Tidefall Academy. She notices inconsistencies, records details other people forget and often gives the group a clearer picture of what actually happened.

Ava Rees's Magic

Ava is strongest when accuracy matters. She tends to refine technique quickly and treats magical progress like a problem that can be measured, tested and improved.

Ava in Tidefall

An old brass key is Ava's signature object, while green is closely associated with her visual identity. Her story belongs to the Tidefall books and Academy setting.

Ava's Connections

Ava is part of Tidefall's central group with Jasper Holloway, Lily Hart and Harper Vale. She is often the person checking whether everyone else's brilliant idea survives contact with basic logic.
