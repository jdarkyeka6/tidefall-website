Ava
Rees

Ava Rees is one of the four central characters of Tidefall. She is a Brannor student at Tidefall Academy and the group's precision-minded investigator: observant, logical and immediately suspicious of a system that almost makes sense. Ava records details other people forget and has a habit of finding the one inconsistency that changes the entire picture.

CHARACTER DOSSIER

Who is Ava Rees?

Role in Tidefall

Ava gives the core four a reliable reality check. She collects facts, spots contradictions and asks whether a theory actually fits the evidence. When a mystery starts becoming a pile of dramatic guesses, Ava is often the person rebuilding it into something testable.

Personality

Ava is precise, observant, organised and logical. She dislikes badly labelled systems, fuzzy explanations and claims that collapse under one obvious question. That does not make her emotionless; it means her instinct is to understand a situation by defining it clearly.

Order: Brannor

Ava belongs to Brannor. Her version of courage is often practical: noticing what is wrong, saying it clearly and helping the group act on accurate information rather than the most exciting theory available.

Visual identity

Green is Ava's main character colour throughout Tidefall artwork and design. Her old brass key is the signature object most closely associated with her.

SIGNATURE OBJECT

Ava's old brass key

A character built to notice what opens

An old brass key is Ava Rees's signature object. The object fits her character particularly well: Ava is drawn to mechanisms, patterns, locked information and the question of what a detail is actually for. In Tidefall imagery, the key acts as a simple visual shorthand for Ava even before the wider meaning of a scene is explained.

MAGIC

Ava's magic

Precision first

Ava is strongest when accuracy matters. She prefers a magical technique she can repeat reliably over a spectacular result she cannot explain. Her instinct is to refine a method until the gap between intention and outcome becomes very small.

Test, adjust, repeat

Ava treats magical progress like a problem that can be measured. If something fails, she wants to know which variable changed. If something succeeds, she wants to know whether it can succeed again.

No wand required

Tidefall magic is not wand-based. Ava casts through Tidefall's wider magical system rather than relying on a wand as a required focus.

Why the group needs her

Precision becomes especially important when Jasper's theories, Harper's speed and Lily's emotional connection to events all point in different directions. Ava can turn a messy situation into a list of things the group actually knows.

CONNECTIONS

Ava's relationships

Jasper Holloway

Jasper and Ava are both observers, but Jasper is more comfortable following strange patterns and possibilities. Ava pushes those possibilities against evidence and asks whether the pattern survives scrutiny.

Lily Hart

Lily's magic and emotions can be intense under pressure, while Ava instinctively breaks problems into smaller pieces. Ava can help make chaos understandable; Lily can remind Ava that not every important thing arrives in a neat category.

Harper Vale

Harper is Ava's clearest opposite. Harper wants to move and learn from the result. Ava wants enough information to predict the result first. Their constant difference in method gives the group both speed and correction.
