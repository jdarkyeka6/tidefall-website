Harper
Vale

Harper Vale is a fictional character in Tidefall, the fantasy universe centred on Tidefall Academy. She is one of Tidefall's four central characters alongside Jasper Holloway, Lily Hart and Ava Rees. Fast-moving, funny and allergic to boring answers, Harper is usually the first person to test a boundary.

Who is Harper Vale?

Harper Vale is a main Tidefall character and Brannor student at Tidefall Academy. She brings momentum to the group, jokes when things get tense and has a talent for turning ordinary school rules into philosophical debates she intends to win.

Harper Vale's Magic

Harper tends toward power before precision. Her challenge is rarely making something happen. It is making exactly the right amount happen.

Harper in Tidefall

Pink is Harper's visual colour across Tidefall artwork. Her story belongs to the Tidefall books and Academy setting documented on this official site.

Harper's Connections

Harper is part of Tidefall's central group with Jasper Holloway, Lily Hart and Ava Rees. Ava's precision and Harper's instinctive energy make them especially entertaining opposites.
