BRANNOR • MAIN TIDEFALL CHARACTER

Harper
Vale

Harper Vale is one of the four central characters in the Tidefall fantasy universe and Tidefall books. Fast-moving, funny and allergic to boring answers, she is usually the first person to test a boundary and just as often the reason everyone else learns where that boundary actually is.

About Harper

Harper brings momentum to the group. She jokes when things get tense, acts quickly, and has a talent for turning ordinary school rules into philosophical debates she absolutely intends to win.

Harper's Magic

Harper tends toward power before precision. Her challenge is rarely making something happen. It is making exactly the right amount happen.

Signature

Pink is Harper's visual colour across Tidefall artwork. Her personal signature item is still deliberately being kept open until the canon choice is locked.

Connections

Harper is part of the central group with Jasper Holloway, Lily Hart and Ava Rees. Ava's precision and Harper's instinctive energy make them especially entertaining opposites.
