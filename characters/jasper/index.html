Jasper
Holloway

Jasper Holloway is one of the four central characters in the Tidefall fantasy universe and Tidefall books. Careful, curious and far more willing to investigate strange things than he probably should be, he notices patterns other people miss, especially when the sea is involved.

About Jasper

Jasper tends to think before he acts, which makes him a useful balance when Tidefall gets chaotic. He is loyal to his friends, persistent with mysteries, and uncomfortable leaving a question unanswered.

Jasper's Magic

Jasper's magic develops around control and awareness. His strangest ability is an unusual sensitivity to the presence beneath Tidefall's waters, something very few people can perceive.

Signature

His necklace is one of the objects most closely associated with him. It follows Jasper through the story as Tidefall becomes increasingly less willing to behave like an ordinary school.

Connections

Jasper is part of Tidefall's central group with Lily Hart, Harper Vale and Ava Rees. The four balance one another in very different ways.
