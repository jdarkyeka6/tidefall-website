Jasper
Holloway

Jasper Holloway is a fictional character in Tidefall, the fantasy universe centred on Tidefall Academy. He is one of Tidefall's four central characters alongside Lily Hart, Harper Vale and Ava Rees. Careful, curious and drawn to mysteries, Jasper notices patterns other people miss, especially when the sea is involved.

Who is Jasper Holloway?

Jasper Holloway is a main Tidefall character and Brannor student at Tidefall Academy. He tends to think before he acts, is loyal to his friends and dislikes leaving a mystery unanswered.

Jasper Holloway's Magic

Jasper's magic develops around control and awareness. His strangest ability is an unusual sensitivity to the presence beneath Tidefall's waters.

Jasper in Tidefall

His necklace is closely associated with him. Jasper's story belongs to the Tidefall books and Academy setting documented on this official site.

Jasper's Connections

Jasper is part of Tidefall's central group with Lily Hart, Harper Vale and Ava Rees. The four balance one another in very different ways.
