Lily Hart is a fictional character in Tidefall, the fantasy universe centred on Tidefall Academy. She is one of Tidefall's four central characters alongside Jasper Holloway, Harper Vale and Ava Rees. Quiet at first and stubborn when it matters, Lily's place at Tidefall is anything but ordinary.

Who is Lily Hart?

Lily Hart is a main Tidefall character and Brannor student at Tidefall Academy. Her confidence grows as she finds people and places where she can choose how she responds instead of simply enduring what happens around her.

Lily Hart's Magic

Lily's magic can become intense under pressure. She also shares a rare sensitivity to the deeper presence connected to Tidefall's waters.

Lily in Tidefall

Her heart hair clip and light-blue visual identity are closely associated with Lily. Her story belongs to the Tidefall books and Academy setting documented on this official site.

Lily's Connections

Lily becomes part of the central group with Jasper Holloway, Harper Vale and Ava Rees. Her arrival changes the shape of the group without replacing what was already there.
