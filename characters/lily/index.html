About

Lily begins more reserved than the others, but that should not be confused with weakness. Her confidence grows as she finds people and places where she can choose how she responds instead of simply enduring what happens around her.

Magic

Lily's magic can become intense under pressure. She also shares the rare ability to sense the deeper presence connected to Tidefall's waters, putting her unusually close to one of the story's biggest mysteries.

Signature

Her heart hair clip is the detail most closely tied to Lily's visual identity, along with the light-blue colour used throughout her Tidefall artwork.

Connections

Lily becomes part of the central group with Jasper Holloway, Harper Vale and Ava Rees. Her arrival changes the shape of the group without replacing what was already there.
