# Tidefall Map Checkpoint v2

## Academy
- Tidefall Academy is built into the cliffs, with towers, bridges and cliffside walkways.
- The Academy is huge.
- The main Academy footprint is based on the approved map shape: a large rounded/circular section with a V-shaped section.
- The V can look flipped depending on the viewing direction. Its orientation is not being defined by a compass direction.
- The Academy is mostly one connected complex, with some detached buildings.
- The specific 500 m measurement is: **back of the castle -> 500 m -> start of the V / wild section**.
- The 500 m measurement is not the total size of the wilderness.

## Harbour and arrival
- Students primarily arrive by boat.
- There is **one main arrival jetty/dock**.
- The main jetty sits inside a sheltered harbour.
- The harbour is carved into/sheltered by the cliffs.
- Stone stairs lead from the harbour up toward the Academy.
- Maintenance/access platforms may exist, but there are not multiple main student arrival jetties.

## Riptide
- The Riptide playing area is roughly the scale of a 7-a-side soccer/hockey field.
- **The entire Riptide facility is underground.**
- It is also underwater, beneath the ocean.
- There is no giant above-ground stadium footprint.
- There is an entrance near the Academy/coast leading underground.
- There is a stretch of open space between the Academy and the Riptide area.
- Riptide is significantly smaller than the Academy.

## Inhabited village
- There is one inhabited village.
- It is on the left/straight route from the Academy in the current map orientation.
- It is roughly **2 km** from the Academy.
- The target travel time is around **5-10 minutes**, rather than a boring 20-minute walk.
- The route should contain things to see and do.
- The world continues beyond the village.

## Abandoned village
- There is a separate abandoned village.
- It is in the opposite direction from the inhabited village.
- The route is mostly **hills, forest and ruins**, with other wilderness/coastal areas mixed in.
- The area should feel wild, ruined and forgotten.

## Wilderness and V
- The V is a major geographical feature of the surrounding wilderness.
- **500 m from the back of the castle is where the V starts.**
- The wilderness continues beyond the initial V.
- The overall playable world is massive.
- If the player can see something, they can generally go there.
- Large distances are fine, but travel should not feel like a walking simulator.
- Important journeys should generally feel like roughly 5-10 minutes of meaningful travel rather than empty walking.
- Later, faster modes of travel can use the same physical routes instead of deleting the geography.

## Overall map philosophy
- The world should feel huge, explorable and geographically believable.
- Travel should contain landmarks, activity and things to discover.
- The map should not be filled with artificial invisible boundaries.
- The geography should remain consistent as more locations are added.

## Locked major layout
- Ocean and cliffs surround the Academy region.
- Main sheltered harbour with **one main arrival jetty**.
- Academy complex around the harbour/cliff area.
- **500 m from the back of the castle to the start of the V/wild section.**
- Riptide is underground and underwater near the coastal side, with a stretch of space between it and the Academy.
- Inhabited village: roughly 2 km away on the left/straight route.
- Abandoned village: opposite direction, reached through mostly hills, forest and ruins with mixed wilderness/coastal areas.
- The wider playable world continues beyond these areas.

## Checkpoint rule
This file is the current map baseline. New map decisions should add to this checkpoint rather than silently changing locked measurements or major locations.
