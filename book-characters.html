THE CORE FOUR

Tidefall Book Characters

Jasper Holloway, Lily Hart, Harper Vale and Ava Rees are the four central characters of the Tidefall Academy fantasy book series. The website gives each of them a full canonical profile, but their biggest moments, choices and character arcs belong to the books.

Meet the four at the centre of the story

Jasper Holloway

Careful, curious and drawn to patterns other people miss. Jasper's connection to Titan-sense makes him central to some of Tidefall's strangest mysteries.

Open Jasper's official profile →

Lily Hart

A late-entry Brannor student whose confidence grows across the story. Lily shares the fuller form of Titan-sense and carries one of Tidefall's most unusual arrivals.

Open Lily's official profile →

Harper Vale

Fast-moving, funny and instinctive. Harper tends to act before everyone else has finished debating and her magic often arrives with power before precision.

Open Harper's official profile →

Ava Rees

Precise, observant and evidence-driven. Ava is the person most likely to turn a messy theory into something the group can actually test.

Open Ava's official profile →

Why the books are the best way to know them

Character pages can tell you what Jasper notices, what Lily carries, how Harper approaches magic or why Ava keeps checking the details. The books show what happens when those traits collide under pressure. That is where friendships are built, mysteries unfold and the Academy stops being a setting and starts becoming part of the problem.

If you found one of these names through search, this page belongs to the original Tidefall Academy fantasy series at tidefall.com.au. The official profiles above are the canonical references for these fictional characters.
