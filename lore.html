Lore Archive

A growing record of Tidefall canon, characters, magic, history and mysteries. Some details remain deliberately unresolved as the series develops.

Core premise

Tidefall Academy stands at the edge of impossible water. Magic is wandless. The sea does not simply obey: the Tide responds. Jasper Holloway, Lily Hart, Harper Vale and Ava Rees sit at the centre of a story involving hidden Academy history, Titans, the Hollow Crown and secrets buried inside Tidefall itself.

The Core Four

Jasper Holloway

Careful, curious and persistent. Jasper notices patterns other people miss. His signature item is a necklace. He possesses the extremely rare ability known as Titan-sense and can hear, sense and communicate with Titans.

Lily Hart

Lily has blonde hair and blue eyes and enters Tidefall unusually late. Before Tidefall she is bullied and struggles with uncontrolled magic, culminating in a magical explosion that leads to her being taken to the Academy. She begins shy and anxious but grows substantially more confident. Lily also possesses full Titan-sense. Her signature item is a heart hair clip.

Lily's Choosing is exceptional: rather than simply following the Tide, she moves against it to reach Brannor, with a dolphin assisting during the event.

Harper Vale

Fast-moving, funny and instinctive, associated with momentum and raw power. During an important early event Harper falls and is rescued by the Tide. Her character colour is pink.

Ava Rees

Observant, logical and precise. Ava approaches mysteries through evidence. Her signature item is an old brass key and her character colour is green.

Tidefall Academy

The Academy is an old stone magical school built around cliffs, ocean and strange tidal access points. Students live, train and study wandless magic there. It contains dormitories, classrooms, hidden areas, underground spaces and corridors whose behaviour and accessibility can be connected to the Tide.

Dormitories can be mixed-gender and are generally built around four students. Some dangerous field locations are restricted to Year 4 students. Students are placed into Orders through the Choosing Tide rather than drawing paper.

Brannor

Brannor is a major Academy Order and the Order associated with the core characters in current visual canon. Lily's entry into Brannor is particularly unusual because she moves against the Choosing Tide.

Magic

Tidefall magic does not use wands. Magic is produced directly by the caster, including through the hands and voice. The Tide is more than ordinary seawater and can respond in ways that appear purposeful.

Titan-sense

Titan-sense is exceptionally rare. Jasper and Lily have the complete known form: they can hear, sense and communicate with Titans. The Headmaster possesses only a partial form, allowing them to hear Titans without speaking back. Only three known people possess Titan-sense in these forms.

Titans and the Crown

Titans are deeply connected to Tidefall's larger mythology and become increasingly important beyond the earliest Academy mysteries. Mordecai, commonly called Mord, is known as the Wearer of the Hollow Crown and is a central antagonist.

Book Five: the Mercer conspiracy

Eli Mercer

Eli Mercer arrives at Tidefall unusually late by harbour boat from outside Tidefall. He arrives without an Order badge and his Choosing is scheduled for the following morning. He is ultimately placed in Nocten alongside Lachlan Vale, Noah Price and Raf Alvarez.

Eli is not simply an ordinary late student. He is connected to a Crown operation targeting Tidefall. He admits involvement in framing Lily Hart, having been assigned to expose Tidefall and gather information from within the Academy.

Hannah Mercer

Hannah Mercer is Eli's mother and directs the investigation. She orders evidence to be planted in Lily's desk and rationalises separating people and manufacturing evidence as necessary to make the case work. Eli's father disagrees with Hannah's actions, creating a fracture inside the Mercer family.

The insider problem

The conspiracy cannot have been carried out by Eli alone. His late admission, access to senior records and knowledge of protected transfer routes require authority he does not possess. This points toward assistance from someone high inside Tidefall's administration.

Eleanor Ward

A major Book Five revelation establishes Hannah Mercer as Eleanor Ward's hidden identity. Even after this is exposed, evidence indicates that another insider remains active within Tidefall.

Relationships and character canon

The core four are designed around a strong friendship rather than constant internal relationship drama. Jasper and Lily eventually develop romantic feelings: Jasper's feelings become part of the story around Book 12, with the relationship progressing into dating around Book 13. Honesty is important to how this relationship is handled.

Selari / Tidefallian

Tidefall's constructed language uses object-subject-verb ordering. Established grammar includes -ra for past tense, -ri for plurals and da- for possession. The earlier future marker was marked for replacement.

Vareth sai Cor’ten. Jae velorin nael, thira ven Tidefall.

Series chronology

The series has been planned on a very large scale, with the earliest five books forming an Academy-focused era before the mythology expands into Titans, kingdoms and larger fractures in the world. Working titles have existed for many future books and remain subject to canon revision.

Canon notes

Older concepts sometimes conflict with newer canon. Astrae/Astraea appeared in earlier Academy material but was later removed from the active core plan. Likewise, older material involving a separate future character named Alex Mercer should not automatically be treated as the same person as Eli Mercer or as proof of a family relationship. This archive prioritises newer established canon when versions conflict.
