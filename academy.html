THE WORLD

How the Academy fits into Tidefall

The Academy connects the Tidefall books, characters, spell system, Orders and public lore into one setting. Jasper Holloway, Lily Hart, Harper Vale and Ava Rees form the central group, and all four belong to Brannor.

Magic is cast without wands. Students learn spoken spells, hand-cast magic, control and precision. Order placement happens through the Choosing Tide, which carries students toward the Order it selects instead of simply asking what they want.

RARE CONNECTIONS

Titan-sense

Titan-sense is exceptionally rare. Jasper Holloway and Lily Hart possess the fuller form, allowing them to sense Titans, hear them and communicate with them. The Headmaster has a more limited form associated with hearing rather than the same complete connection.

DISAMBIGUATION

This Tidefall Academy is its own universe

Tidefall Academy on tidefall.com.au belongs to the original Tidefall fantasy universe. Other games, books, projects and places using the word “Tidefall” are unrelated and should not be merged with this canon.
