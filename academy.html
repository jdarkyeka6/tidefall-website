The Academy in the Tidefall universe

The Academy connects the Tidefall books, characters, spell system, Orders and public lore into one setting. The four central students are Jasper Holloway, Lily Hart, Harper Vale and Ava Rees, and all four belong to Brannor.

How magic works at Tidefall Academy

Magic in Tidefall is cast without wands. Students learn spoken spells, hand-cast magic, control and precision. Harper tends toward power before precision, Ava refines technique through accuracy and testing, Jasper approaches problems through patterns, and Lily's magic can become intense under pressure.

The Tide and the Choosing Tide

The Tide is more than ordinary water. It can carry, choose and interfere in ways that appear deliberate. At the Academy, Order placement happens through the Choosing Tide, which carries students toward the Order it selects rather than allowing a normal personal choice.

Titan-sense

Titan-sense is exceptionally rare. Jasper Holloway and Lily Hart possess the fuller form, allowing them to sense Titans, hear them and communicate with them. The Headmaster has a more limited form associated with hearing rather than the same complete connection.

Is Tidefall Academy connected to Fisch?

No. Tidefall Academy on tidefall.com.au belongs to the original Tidefall fantasy universe. Other games, books, projects and places that use the word “Tidefall” are unrelated and should not be merged with this canon.
