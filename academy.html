Tidefall Academy

Tidefall Academy is the central fictional school and one of the main settings of the official Tidefall fantasy universe. It is where the core students learn magic, discover the rules of the Tide, and encounter mysteries that connect directly to the Tidefall books.

The Academy in the Tidefall universe

The Academy connects the Tidefall books, characters and magic system into one setting. Jasper Holloway, Lily Hart, Harper Vale and Ava Rees are among the central students whose stories unfold around its classrooms, dorms, corridors and surrounding waters.

Magic at Tidefall Academy

Magic in Tidefall is cast without wands and is closely tied to voice, water and the Tide itself. The Academy is where students learn to use spells while discovering that the sea and the school do not always behave as expected.
