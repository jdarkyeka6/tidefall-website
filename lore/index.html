PEOPLE & PERSONALITY

How the four fit together

Jasper: the pattern-finder

Jasper tends to slow mysteries down and look for the detail that does not fit. He is curious, loyal and persistent, especially when the sea or the Academy behaves in a way everyone else is ready to dismiss.

Lily: the late arrival

Lily begins shy and anxious after a difficult life before Tidefall, including bullying and an uncontrolled magical incident. At Tidefall she grows into someone increasingly willing to choose her own response instead of simply enduring what happens around her.

Harper: the momentum

Harper acts quickly, jokes under pressure and tests boundaries almost automatically. Her speed can create trouble, but it also prevents the group from becoming trapped in endless hesitation.

Ava: the precision

Ava notices inconsistencies, remembers details and wants systems to make sense. She is the person most likely to ask which part of a dramatic theory is actually supported by evidence.

MAGIC & THE TIDE

Magic, Titan-sense and the Choosing Tide

Magic without wands

Tidefall magic is not a wand-based system. Students cast directly through Tidefall's magical rules rather than requiring a wand. Different characters show different strengths: Harper leans toward raw power, Ava toward precision, Lily can become extremely intense under pressure, and Jasper's story is strongly connected to awareness and the Titans.

Titan-sense

Titan-sense is an exceptionally rare ability connected to Titans. Jasper Holloway and Lily Hart possess the fuller form, allowing them to sense, hear and communicate with Titans. The Headmaster has a more limited form associated with hearing.

The Choosing Tide

Order placement is performed by the Choosing Tide. Rather than students simply selecting a house or drawing a random piece of paper, the Tide carries each student toward the Order it chooses.

Lily's unusual placement

Lily's late placement does not proceed normally. The Tide begins pulling her elsewhere, Lily resists and keeps swimming against it, and a dolphin becomes part of the chaotic crossing before she reaches Brannor.

SOURCE OF TRUTH

What counts as official Tidefall canon?

The Tidefall universe documented on tidefall.com.au is an original fantasy setting centred on Tidefall Academy, its characters, magic, mysteries and the Tide. Other books, games, places or projects that also use the word Tidefall are unrelated unless this website explicitly says otherwise.

For character questions, the dedicated character pages are the most specific public canon source. This lore archive provides the shared context that connects those character facts together.

QUICK REFERENCE

Tidefall lore FAQ

Who are the main Tidefall characters?

Jasper Holloway, Lily Hart, Harper Vale and Ava Rees are the four central characters.

What Order are the core four in?

All four are in Brannor.

Who has Titan-sense?

Jasper Holloway and Lily Hart possess the fuller form. The Headmaster has a more limited form associated with hearing.

Can Jasper and Lily talk to Titans?

Yes. Their fuller Titan-sense allows them to sense, hear and communicate with Titans.

Does Tidefall use wands?

No. Tidefall magic is cast without wands.

How are students placed into Orders?

Through the Choosing Tide, which carries students toward the Order it chooses.

Why is Lily's arrival unusual?

She enters the Academy later than most students after an uncontrolled magical incident, and her Choosing Tide placement becomes an unusual struggle against the Tide before she reaches Brannor.

Are Jasper and Lily together?

They are an eventual later-series pairing. Their relationship develops gradually while the friendship of the core four remains central.
