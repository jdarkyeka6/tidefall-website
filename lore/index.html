MAGIC & THE TIDE

Magic system and mystical forces

Magic without wands

Tidefall magic is not a wand-based system. Students cast directly through Tidefall's magical rules and the power of the Tide.

Titan-sense

Titan-sense is an exceptionally rare ability connected to Titans. Jasper Holloway and Lily Hart possess the fuller form, allowing them to sense and communicate with Titans.

The Choosing Tide

Order placement is performed by the Choosing Tide. Rather than students choosing, the Tide carries students toward the Order it selects.

The Five Classes

Spells are organized into five classes: Lumen (light), Tide (water), Vela (air), Aegis (defense) and Ember (fire/heat).

SOURCE OF TRUTH

What counts as official Tidefall canon?

The Tidefall universe documented on tidefall.com.au is an original fantasy setting centred on Tidefall Academy, its characters, magic, mysteries and the Tide.

For character questions, the dedicated character pages are the most specific public canon source. This lore archive provides the shared context connecting those character facts to the broader universe.
