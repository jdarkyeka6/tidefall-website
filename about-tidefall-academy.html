OFFICIAL SERIES OVERVIEW

About Tidefall Academy

Tidefall Academy is a children’s fantasy book series set around a magical academy where students learn wandless magic, live in dorms, join Orders and uncover mysteries connected to the Tide. The story follows four central students: Jasper Holloway, Lily Hart, Harper Vale and Ava Rees.

What kind of series is Tidefall Academy?

It is a magical-school fantasy with adventure, friendship, mystery and an expanding world. Academy life is part of the story, but the series also explores the wider history, abilities, creatures and mysteries surrounding Tidefall.

Magical academy

Students learn and practise magic through classes, rules, traditions, dorm life and the Academy’s Orders.

Wandless magic

Magic in Tidefall is cast without wands and includes spells, hand-cast magic and rare abilities.

Four central characters

Jasper, Lily, Harper and Ava approach the Academy differently while forming the core of the continuing story.

Ongoing mystery

The world and its larger questions develop across the books rather than existing only inside separate adventures.

Who is Tidefall Academy written for?

The series is designed for developing young readers who want a larger fantasy story than a simple beginner chapter book. Confident readers can read independently, while children still building confidence may enjoy reading with a parent and getting occasional help with more difficult words or fantasy terminology.

The central students

Jasper Holloway, Lily Hart, Harper Vale and Ava Rees form the main group. Their personalities and approaches to problems differ, giving the series room for friendship, disagreement, teamwork and individual growth without making one character carry the entire story.

Tidefall Academy itself

The Academy is more than a backdrop. Its classes, Orders, Choosing Tide, dorms, rules and traditions shape how students learn and how the story unfolds. The school connects the everyday experience of being a student with the deeper magical history of the Tidefall universe.
