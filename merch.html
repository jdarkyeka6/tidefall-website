DESIGN LAW

Locked visual rules

  • Character colours: Lily light blue, Harper pink, Ava green, Jasper warm gold-orange.
  • Hoodies must look thick, soft and puffy, never like flat print-on-demand blanks.
  • Fronts stay restrained. Backs can carry the larger world-building graphics.
  • No giant character portraits on clothing.
  • All product images use one backdrop, crop, lighting setup and garment proportion system.
  • Pieces must still look wearable to someone who has never heard of Tidefall.

RELEASE SYSTEM

Foundation first.

Drop 001 becomes the permanent base range. Brannor, book-specific pieces, school gear, tech, collectibles and the enormous existing catalogue become later drops instead of flooding launch day.
