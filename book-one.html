Book One begins the Tidefall Academy series with Jasper Holloway and the world that will bring Jasper, Lily Hart, Harper Vale and Ava Rees together around impossible water, wandless magic and an Academy that is keeping more secrets than it admits.

WHY THE BOOKS MATTER

The website is the world. The books are the story.

You can take quizzes, explore rooms, learn spells and dig through public lore across tidefall.com.au, but those pages are designed to support the fiction rather than replace it. The books are the main narrative of Tidefall Academy, with the character arcs, discoveries, danger and long-running mysteries unfolding there first.

Magic without wandsMagic is learned through control, spoken spells, hand-casting and the strange influence of the Tide.
A school that does not sit stillTidefall Academy is full of hidden routes, unusual traditions, Orders and water that sometimes behaves with intent.
Four central charactersJasper, Lily, Harper and Ava approach the same mysteries in completely different ways.
