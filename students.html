About the Tidefall character list

This is the official character guide for the fantasy universe published at tidefall.com.au. Jasper, Lily, Harper and Ava form Tidefall's central group, with additional students appearing throughout Tidefall Academy and the wider story.

The name Tidefall is also used by unrelated games, books and projects. Their characters are separate from this universe. For this Tidefall, the canonical character names and profiles are published here and linked directly below.
