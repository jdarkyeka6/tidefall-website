LIFE INSIDE THE ACADEMY

Tidefall Academy Dorms

Dorms are part of everyday life at Tidefall Academy. Students live alongside others from the Academy, giving the setting a life outside lessons, formal training and major events.

Shared student spaces

Dorm life gives students somewhere to study, talk, plan, argue over small things, recover after difficult training and simply exist as friends. The core four are not defined only by adventure or magic, so the Academy includes ordinary routines as well as unusual ones.

Orders and dorm identity

Order membership is a major part of Academy identity. Jasper Holloway, Lily Hart, Harper Vale and Ava Rees are all connected to Brannor, an Order associated with courage, loyalty and action.

Mixed student life

Tidefall Academy dorms are mixed-gender shared student spaces. The emphasis is on friendship, routines and Academy life rather than romance-driven dorm drama.

Why dorms matter to the setting

The dorms help make Tidefall Academy feel like a place students actually live in, not just a backdrop for magic. They connect character relationships, personal items, study habits and Order identity without requiring the site to reveal plot outcomes.
