THE OFFICIAL INTRODUCTION

What is Tidefall?

Tidefall is an original fantasy book series and interactive fantasy universe centred on Tidefall Academy. It follows four main students, Jasper Holloway, Lily Hart, Harper Vale and Ava Rees, through a world shaped by water, magic, choice and mysteries connected to the sea.

The story

The books are the main path through Tidefall. They follow the students of Tidefall Academy as they learn magic, face the strange forces surrounding the school and make choices that shape what happens next.

The four main characters

Jasper Holloway, Lily Hart, Harper Vale and Ava Rees are the four main students featured at the centre of the Tidefall universe.

Tidefall Academy

Tidefall Academy is the central school setting of the series, with dorms, classrooms, hidden corridors, old stone, impossible water and secrets scattered throughout the grounds.

Magic and the sea

Tidefall's identity is built around water and magic. The official site includes voice-cast spells and hidden discoveries that expand on the world beyond the books.

Is Tidefall a game?

No. This Tidefall is first and foremost a fantasy book series and fictional universe. The website includes interactive features, but the books are the main story path.

Where is the official Tidefall website?

The official website is tidefall.com.au, where readers can explore the books, students, Tidefall Academy, spells and interactive features.
