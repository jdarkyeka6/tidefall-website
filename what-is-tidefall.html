What Is Tidefall?

Tidefall is an original fantasy universe shaped by water, magic and choice. Its central setting is Tidefall Academy, and its main story is told through the official Tidefall books.

The Tidefall described on this website is a distinct fictional universe. Other unrelated works, games or uses may share the name “Tidefall”, but they are separate from this universe.

Tidefall Academy

Tidefall Academy is the central school in the Tidefall universe. Its dorms, classrooms, hidden corridors, impossible waters and traditions form the main setting for the students' stories.

The Tidefall Books

The Tidefall books are the main path through the universe. They follow Jasper Holloway, Lily Hart, Harper Vale and Ava Rees as they move through Tidefall Academy and discover how deeply the sea, magic and the Academy are connected.

Main Characters

Jasper Holloway, Lily Hart, Harper Vale and Ava Rees are the four central students of Tidefall. Each character has a different relationship with the Academy, magic and the choices the Tide places in front of them.

Magic in Tidefall

Magic in Tidefall is performed without wands. Spoken spells, hand-cast magic, the Tide itself and stranger abilities all belong to the same universe explored across the books and official site.
