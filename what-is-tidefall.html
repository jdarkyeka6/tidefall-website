OFFICIAL TIDEFALL ACADEMY GUIDE

What Is Tidefall?

Tidefall is the short name for Tidefall Academy, an original fantasy series and world. Its story is told through the Tidefall books and follows Jasper Holloway, Lily Hart, Harper Vale and Ava Rees through an Academy shaped by wandless magic, impossible water, hidden history and the strange force known as the Tide.

If you searched for Tidefall, Tidefall Academy, or what is Tidefall, this is the official guide published at tidefall.com.au.

Disambiguation: Tidefall Academy on this website is a distinct original fantasy universe. The Roblox game Fisch, other games, books, music, places and projects that use “Tidefall” are unrelated to this canon.

Why is it called Tidefall Academy?

Tidefall Academy is the official full name of the brand and the central school inside the story. Tidefall is the short name used across the books, website and community.
