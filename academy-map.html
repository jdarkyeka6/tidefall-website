ACADEMY LOCATION GUIDE

Tidefall Academy Map Guide

Tidefall Academy is more than one building. Its important spaces include classrooms, dorms, corridors, shared areas, grounds and surrounding waters. This guide describes the layout categories without revealing secret routes or story-critical locations.

Main areas

  • Teaching spaces for magical practice, instruction and controlled exercises.
  • Dorm areas where students live, study and spend time outside lessons.
  • Corridors and shared spaces connecting the Academy and creating room for exploration.
  • Grounds used for movement, gatherings and supervised activities.
  • Surrounding waters, which matter because the Tide is an active part of Academy life rather than ordinary scenery.

Why water matters to the layout

The Tide influences placement and other parts of the world, so water around the Academy is not simply decorative. The Choosing Tide is one of the clearest examples of the Academy's geography and magic overlapping.
