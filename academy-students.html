OFFICIAL CHARACTER GUIDE

Tidefall Academy Students

The central Tidefall story follows four students at Tidefall Academy. This page focuses on who they are at the Academy without revealing major book outcomes.

Jasper Holloway

Careful, curious, observant and persistent. Jasper is a pattern-finder who often notices connections other people miss. He is a Brannor student and possesses the fuller form of Titan-sense.

Lily Hart

Caring, stubborn when it matters and initially cautious. Lily joins the Academy later than most students, belongs to Brannor and also possesses the fuller form of Titan-sense.

Harper Vale

Energetic, bold, direct and instinctive. Harper creates momentum and tends to test boundaries. Her magic leans toward power before precision. She is a Brannor student.

Ava Rees

Precise, logical, organised and detail-focused. Ava checks evidence and refines technique quickly. She is a Brannor student whose magic is strongest when accuracy matters.

Why the group works

The four approach problems differently. Jasper follows patterns and possibilities, Lily brings emotional and intuitive awareness, Harper acts quickly and Ava tests evidence. Those differences shape how they learn and explore the Academy.

Brannor

All four central students are members of Brannor, an Order associated with courage, loyalty and action. Order placement is determined through the Choosing Tide.
