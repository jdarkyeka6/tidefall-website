Tidefall Books Reading Order

Start with Tidefall Book One. Tidefall Academy is built as a book-first fantasy universe. The website is full of lore, characters, maps, quizzes and interactive extras, but none of those need to come before the books.

Read Book 1

As later books become publicly available, this page will remain the official place to check the reading order rather than relying on unrelated projects that also use the name Tidefall.

Books first, extras second

The Academy pages explain how Tidefall works. The character profiles give you clean reference material. The lore archive connects things like Titan-sense, Brannor and the Choosing Tide. The interactive pages let you play inside the setting. But the narrative itself belongs to the books.

If you want the least-spoiler route, read the books first, then use the website whenever you want to look something up or explore beyond the chapter you just finished.

What can I read right now?

Is Tidefall Academy the same as Tidefall?

Yes. Tidefall Academy is the official full name and Tidefall is the short name. The books, Academy, characters and official world at tidefall.com.au all belong to the same original fantasy property.
