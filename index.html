MAGIC & SPELLS

The Tidefall spell system

Magic in Tidefall flows without wands. Students cast spells directly, channeling the Tide's power. Spells are grouped into five classes: Lumen (light), Tide (water), Vela (air), Aegis (defense) and Ember (fire).
