Jasper thought. Magic could move a feather. Heat water. Build shields. Record memory. Carry a message through a mirror. It could also be used badly. Overused. Misread. Made unnecessarily complicated when hands would work. And some things he had once called magic were not ordinary magic at all. Water was not a spell. Water was a being, or presence, or something for which Tidefall did not yet have a clean word. He wrote: Magic is a way people can change things, but being able to change something doesn't mean you understand it or should change it. He reread it. Very Tidefall. Maybe very him. Harper wrote: Magic is useful if you don't act stupid with it. Ward read it over her shoulder.

Names carved into a bench before carving names into benches became prohibited. A rainwater channel widened after an old flood. A modern accessibility ramp crossing an older set of steps. The academy looked permanent from the sea. Up close, it was layers of decisions.
