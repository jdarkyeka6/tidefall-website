CHAPTER SIXTY-FIVE

Friendship did not make people automatically honest. Jasper learned this near the end of first year. He should have learned it earlier. The problem began with Ava's notebook. Harper found Jasper's missing shoe under her own bed. Nobody understood how it got there. Some mysteries stayed unsolved because they were stupid.

CHAPTER SIXTY-SEVEN
THE CHRONICLE

Three weeks turned out to be enough time for everyone at Tidefall to become unbearable about Orders. Older students made predictions. First-years compared guesses. Even staff occasionally said things that sounded suspiciously like opinions before correcting themselves.

A Calyx student told Jasper he seemed suited to restoration because he liked shields. A Velmor fourth-year argued that his curiosity made him obvious Velmor. Brannor students noticed the storm story. Nocten students noticed Ava's notebook and began claiming her by cultural right. "Exactly."

To escape the noise, the trio started a Year One Chronicle. It began as a single page in a blank book Soren donated from the library surplus shelf. The rule was simple: anyone in first year could add one memory, fact, joke or lesson from the year, provided it did not reveal somebody else's private information. The book filled quickly.

ROOM FOURTEEN INVENTED BAG BED AND REGRETS NOTHING.
