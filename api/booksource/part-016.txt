CHAPTER FIFTY-ONE

CHAPTER FIFTY-TWO

By Monday, the storm had a story. Stories formed faster than repairs. The official version was simple. A stronger-than-forecast coastal front struck Tidefall during ongoing breakwater work. CHAPTER FIFTY-THREE

The lower harbour reopened twelve days after the storm. Year One visited under supervision as part of the investigation review.
